baking

* How did you get to start baking?

I just always love food and eating, so I was naturally interested. I tried following a recipe once and the result was impressive—plus, I felt like I had a knack for it! So, I taught myself.

* What’s the most memorable process of baking dessert?

Seeing the finished product rise beautifully in the oven—and the smell is amazing, too. 😛

* Do you have any personal habits or methods when you bake?

Baking that’s healthy but also delicious—in other words: Minimizing butter and sugar as much as possible without sacrificing flavor! That’s my baking philosophy. 😼

* What’s the most memorable thing you’ve ever baked?

The tiramisu we made together. Thanks to your expert assistance and our successful recipe, we baked the ultimate tiramisu. 🍰
